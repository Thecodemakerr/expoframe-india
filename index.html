Senior Event Photographer

📍 Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore 💼 Full-time 💰 ₹6-10 LPA

Experience: 3-5 years in event photography

Requirements:

  • Proficiency with DSLR/Mirrorless cameras (Canon/Sony)
  • Experience with studio and natural lighting
  • Strong portfolio of event/exhibition photography
  • Willingness to travel frequently
  • Knowledge of Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop

Videographer & Editor

📍 Bangalore, Hyderabad 💼 Full-time 💰 ₹5-8 LPA

Experience: 2-4 years in video production

Requirements:

  • Experience with professional video cameras (Sony FX series, Blackmagic)
  • Proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects
  • Strong storytelling and editing skills
  • Ability to work under tight deadlines
  • Knowledge of color grading and audio mixing

Drone Operator & Aerial Photographer

📍 All Major Cities 💼 Contract/Freelance 💰 ₹500-1000 per hour

Experience: 1-3 years in drone operations

Requirements:

  • DGCA certified drone pilot license
  • Experience with DJI drones (Mavic, Inspire series)
  • Strong aerial photography and videography skills
  • Knowledge of airspace regulations
  • Ability to work in challenging weather conditions

Photo Editor & Retoucher

📍 Remote/Mumbai 💼 Full-time 💰 ₹4-6 LPA

Experience: 2-3 years in photo editing

Requirements:

  • Expert-level knowledge of Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop
  • Strong color correction and retouching skills
  • Ability to process high volumes of images quickly
  • Attention to detail and quality standards
  • Experience with batch processing and automation

Client Relations Manager

📍 Mumbai 💼 Full-time 💰 ₹8-12 LPA

Experience: 4-6 years in client management

Requirements:

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Experience in event management or photography industry
  • Strong negotiation and sales abilities
  • Ability to manage multiple clients simultaneously
  • Fluency in English and Hindi (regional languages preferred)

Junior Photographer (Fresher)

📍 Chennai, Kolkata 💼 Full-time 💰 ₹3-4 LPA

Experience: 0-1 years (Fresh graduates welcome)

Requirements:

  • Diploma/Degree in Photography or related field
  • Basic knowledge of camera operations and composition
  • Passion for event and exhibition photography
  • Willingness to learn and grow
  • Portfolio showcasing your best work (even personal projects)
